# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Still is an open-source project. We want everyone who uses, discusses, or contributes to it to be treated with respect, regardless of experience, identity, background, or viewpoint.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate in issues, pull requests, reviews, and project-related discussions.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Explain disagreements calmly and assume good faith where reasonable.
- Accept constructive feedback and correct mistakes when they are pointed out.
- Share only information you have the right to share, and respect other people's privacy.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, threats, discrimination, slurs, personal attacks, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Do not publish another person's private information or encourage others to target them.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies to project spaces, including repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces where someone represents Still.

The maintainer may edit or remove harmful contributions, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary. Responses will be proportionate to the behaviour and the circumstances.

## Reporting a concern

Contact the maintainer through the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/aabhro). Please do not post sensitive details in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed as carefully and discreetly as possible.

Thank you for helping keep Still welcoming and constructive.
